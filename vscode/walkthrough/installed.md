# What just got installed

The Valency extension added two things to VS Code — and runs nothing on your machine:

- **The Valency MCP server connection** — a pointer to the hosted server at `https://mcp.valency.io/`, which exposes the full Valency research-corpus tool surface: semantic search, author profiles, collaboration networks, publication trends, and more.
- **Seven research skills** your agent invokes automatically based on what you ask:
  - `valency-profile` — profile a researcher's work, areas, and collaborators
  - `valency-landscape` — map a research field's key players and subdomains
  - `valency-similar` — explore the neighborhood around a known paper
  - `valency-trends` — chart how research topics grow and change over time
  - `valency-network` — map co-author networks and research divergence
  - `valency-reading-list` — build a curated reading list around a researcher
  - `valency-fresh-collaborators` — find relevant researchers outside an existing network

All analysis runs against the hosted Valency corpus of tens of millions of research papers and preprints.
